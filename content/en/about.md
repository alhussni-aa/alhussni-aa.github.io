Hi, I am **Abdullah Alhussni**, a senior at **New York University Abu Dhabi** completing a **BSc in Electrical Engineering and Mathematics**.

For my senior capstone under the supervision of Professor Marwa Chafii and Dr. Roberto Bomfin, I am conducting research on machine learning-enabled multiband wireless sensing for activity recognition. My work focuses on developing a robust, privacy-preserving Human Activity Recognition (HAR) system that leverages multi-band Channel State Information (CSI) from the FR3 spectrum. By exploiting the complementary propagation characteristics of bands between 7 GHz and 24 GHz, I am building a system more resilient to environmental variation than traditional single-band models. This project involves a Software-Defined Radio (SDR) platform using USRP X310 units to collect synchronized CSI streams, and encompasses the full pipeline from hardware optimization and signal preprocessing to training machine learning models for activity classification.

I recently worked with the **Gelfand Research Group**, contributing to the analysis of variable sources in the dwarf galaxy IC 10 using Python and Astropy. Previously, I spent over a year at the **Center for Quantum and Topological Systems (CQTS)**, where my work ranged from writing proofs in Cubical Agda to NV center NMR/ESR spectroscopy hardware. During this time, I also co-authored a peer-reviewed publication on dynamic nuclear polarization.

I am particularly driven by the intersection of hardware and mathematical optimization. Whether I am working at the micro-scale of FPGA microprocessor architecture and embedded systems or the macro-scale of power electronics and renewable energy, I enjoy using my background in both engineering and mathematics to design efficient, high-performance systems.

Outside of my technical research, I am deeply committed to community leadership and institutional service. I serve as the President of the Lantern Subcommittee under the Coexistence Board, where I lead initiatives to support and advocate for Muslim identity on campus through inclusive programming. Additionally, I contribute to the university's growth through my work with the Admissions office and student mentoring.
