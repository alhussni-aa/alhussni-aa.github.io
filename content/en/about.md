I am an Electrical Engineering and Mathematics graduate from **NYU Abu Dhabi**, starting the Erasmus Mundus Joint Master’s program in Microwave Electronics and Photonics (**EMIMEP**) in September 2026.

My technical background sits at the intersection of hardware and mathematical optimization, including FPGA design, embedded systems, and power electronics. For my capstone project with Prof. Marwa Chafii and Dr. Roberto Bomfin, I built a privacy-preserving activity recognition system from the ground up, configuring Software-Defined Radios to capture multi-band Channel State Information across the FR3 spectrum and training models to exploit cross-band propagation.

Before that, I spent over a year working on quantum technologies at the Center for Quantum and Topological Systems. I started out writing formal proofs in Cubical Agda for topological quantum computing before moving to the Quantum eXploration Lab. There, I optimized DNP pulse sequences using optimal control theory, which led to a co-authored publication, and helped assemble a quantum diamond spectrometer. My research experience also extends to observational astrophysics with the Gelfand Research Group and industrial automation through a controls engineering internship at ASEC Automation in Cairo.

Outside the lab, I was heavily involved in student life at NYUAD. I served as a Lead Ambassador for Admissions, where I managed campus tours, ambassador recruitment, and Candidate Weekend logistics. I also served as President of the Lantern Subcommittee under the Coexistence Board, leading initiatives to support Muslim student identity and build inclusive programming on campus.
