Hi, I'm **Abdullah Alhussni**, a senior at **NYU Abu Dhabi** studying **Electrical Engineering and Mathematics**.

My capstone research, under Prof. Marwa Chafii and Dr. Roberto Bomfin, develops a privacy-preserving activity recognition system using multi-band Channel State Information from the FR3 spectrum. The project covers the full pipeline, from a Software-Defined Radio platform for synchronized data collection, through signal preprocessing, to training machine learning classifiers that exploit complementary propagation characteristics across bands.

I spent over a year at the **Center for Quantum and Topological Systems (CQTS)**, first writing formal proofs in Cubical Agda for topological quantum computing, then moving into the Quantum eXploration Lab where I optimized Dynamic Nuclear Polarization pulse sequences on HPC clusters using optimal control theory, and helped assemble a quantum diamond spectrometer for NV-center spectroscopy. I also co-authored a peer-reviewed publication on dynamic nuclear polarization. I worked with the **Gelfand Research Group** as well, analyzing variable sources in dwarf galaxy IC 10 by cross-matching LCO observations, constructing light curves, and generating photometric diagrams to classify source types.

I'm drawn to problems at the intersection of hardware and mathematical optimization, whether that's FPGA design, embedded systems, or power electronics. Outside the lab, I serve as a Lead Ambassador for budgeting and scheduling at NYU Abu Dhabi Admissions, where I manage campus tours, student panels, Candidate Weekend logistics, and ambassador recruitment and training. I also serve as the President of the Lantern Subcommittee under the Coexistence Board, where I lead initiatives to support Muslim identity on campus through inclusive programming.
