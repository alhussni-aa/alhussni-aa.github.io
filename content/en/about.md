Hi, I'm **Abdullah Alhussni** — a recent graduate (May 2026) of **NYU Abu Dhabi** with a B.Sc. in **Electrical Engineering and Mathematics**.

My capstone, under Prof. Marwa Chafii and Dr. Roberto Bomfin, built a privacy-preserving activity recognition system using multi-band Channel State Information from the FR3 spectrum, covering the full pipeline from SDR data collection through signal preprocessing to ML classification. I also spent over a year at the **Center for Quantum and Topological Systems (CQTS)**, working on topological quantum computing, optimizing Dynamic Nuclear Polarization pulse sequences on HPC clusters, and co-authoring a publication on quantum sensing. Separately, I worked with the **Gelfand Research Group** analyzing variable stars in dwarf galaxy IC 10.

I'm drawn to problems at the intersection of hardware and mathematical optimization — FPGA design, embedded systems, and power electronics. Outside the lab, I served as Lead Ambassador at NYU Abu Dhabi Admissions and President of the Lantern Subcommittee (MSA). In September, I'll begin my M.Sc. through the **Erasmus Mundus Joint Master (EMIMEP)** in Microwave Electronics and Photonics across the University of Limoges and the University of Brescia.
