Hi, I am **Abdullah Alhussni**.

I am a senior at **New York University Abu Dhabi** completing a **BSc in Electrical Engineering and Mathematics** (GPA: 3.641, expected May 2026). My capstone project is on **Integrated Sensing and Communication (ISAC) for 6G Networks** under Prof. Marwa Chafii.

I currently work as a Research Assistant at the **Gelfand Center for Sustainable Energy**, designing and simulating oscillating water column energy harvesters under Prof. Matthew Wagers. Previously, I spent over a year at the **Center for Quantum and Topological Systems (CQTS)** contributing to topological quantum computing research — from writing proofs in Cubical Agda to NV center spectroscopy hardware.

I enjoy working at the intersection of hardware and software: circuit design in Altium and Cadence, FPGA microprocessor architecture in VHDL, embedded systems, and applied machine learning. Outside of engineering, I stay involved through campus leadership, admissions work, and mentoring.

**Highlighted Coursework:** Electronics, Applied Machine Learning, Applied Optimization, Control Systems Engineering, Analog & Digital Communication Theory, Advanced Digital Logic, Real-Time Embedded Systems, Design & Innovation, Probability & Statistics.
