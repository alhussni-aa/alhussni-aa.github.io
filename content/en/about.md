Hi, I am **Abdullah Alhussni**.

I am a senior at **New York University Abu Dhabi** completing a **BSc in Electrical Engineering and Mathematics** (GPA: 3.641, expected May 2026).

## Research

My capstone project is on **Integrated Sensing and Communication (ISAC) for 6G Networks** under Prof. Marwa Chafii -- specifically, machine learning-enabled multiband wireless sensing for activity recognition.

I currently work as a Research Assistant with the **Gelfand Research Group**, contributing to the analysis of variable sources in dwarf galaxy IC 10 using Python and Astropy.

Previously, I spent over a year at the **Center for Quantum and Topological Systems (CQTS)** contributing to topological quantum computing research -- from writing proofs in Cubical Agda to NV center NMR/ESR spectroscopy hardware -- and co-authored a peer-reviewed publication on dynamic nuclear polarization.

## Skills & Interests

I enjoy working at the intersection of hardware and software: circuit design in Altium and Cadence, FPGA microprocessor architecture in VHDL, embedded systems with STM32 and ESP32, and applied machine learning with PyTorch.

Outside of engineering, I stay involved through campus leadership (MSA President), admissions work, and mentoring.
